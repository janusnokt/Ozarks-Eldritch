Questions people ask

Is this a ghost tour?

Not quite. Eureka Springs has ghosts enough, though this walk travels older roads. We follow springs, thresholds, second sight, strange geography, and stories about the beings people once called the Good Folk, the Hidden People, or the Kindly Neighbors.

Do I need to believe in any of it?

No. Bring belief, disbelief, curiosity, or nothing at all. The walk asks nothing of your cosmology. We work with folklore, attention, place, and the old human habit of finding meaning where water touches stone.

What will I be asked to do?

Walk. Listen. Notice. At several points we may invite you into simple practices of attention: peripheral vision, contemplative listening, light entrancement. You stay awake, aware, and free to decline anything.

Can children come?

We built the night for adults and older teens. Younger guests may enjoy the stories, though the walking, the darkness, and the quieter exercises ask for patience.

What if the weather turns?

Light weather joins the atmosphere. Dangerous weather ends the night. If conditions make the walk unsafe, we reschedule and say so at once.
